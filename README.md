\# 🏥 Hospital Management System



\## 📌 Overview



The \*\*Hospital Management System\*\* is a software application designed to simplify and manage hospital operations efficiently. It helps manage patient information, doctor details, appointments, medical records, and other essential hospital activities through a centralized system.



The main objective of this project is to reduce manual paperwork, improve data management, and make hospital services more organized and accessible.



\## ✨ Features



\- \*\*Patient Management:\*\* Add, update, view, and manage patient information.

\- \*\*Doctor Management:\*\* Maintain doctor profiles, specializations, and availability.

\- \*\*Appointment Management:\*\* Schedule, view, and manage patient appointments.

\- \*\*Medical Records:\*\* Maintain patient medical history and treatment details.

\- \*\*User Authentication:\*\* Provide secure access to authorized users.

\- \*\*Admin Dashboard:\*\* Manage hospital information and monitor activities.

\- \*\*Billing Management:\*\* Maintain patient bills and payment details, if implemented.

\- \*\*Search Functionality:\*\* Quickly find patient and doctor information.
